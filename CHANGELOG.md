- Default-pack alphabet policy (`data-builder/src/alphabet.rs`, `docs/lexicon-review.md`): an explicit human project policy that a lexical entry of the default/reviewed pack consists only of the 31 letters of the Kurmancî alphabet (`a b c ç d e ê f g h i î j k l m n o p q r s ş t u û v w x y z`), applied mechanically to the normalized form. Digits, superscripts and symbols are not eligible; alphabetic characters outside the alphabet are not eligible; hyphen and apostrophes are deliberately left to a separate human-reviewed tokenization policy. - Query-time candidate index (`engine/src/index.rs`): `suggest`, `correct` and `complete` no longer scan every lexicon entry. The engine builds at load a sorted normalized-form index (binary search instead of the linear lookup per trie prefix hit) and a second compact trie over diacritic-stripped forms, walked with a unit-cost optimal-string-alignment bound of 2 (`Trie::fuzzy_terminals`). The walk yields a proven superset of every entry the scorer can accept (every non-diacritic edit costs at least 0.75, so at most two fit under the 2.0 threshold; proof in the module), and the candidates are re-scored by the same stage functions the reference uses, in ascending lexicon order, so results, kinds, edit costs, ordering and counts are byte-identical. The full scan is retained crate-privately as `Engine::suggest_reference_full_scan` as the oracle; `query_equivalence_tests` brute-forces the pruning lemma, asserts the superset invariant and compares the ranked output on a synthetic lexicon and on the reviewed and experimental packs over benchmark and QA inputs, every evaluation-suite input, a deterministic near-miss corpus and Unicode edge cases (the real-pack suite runs in release mode as its own CI step; `KURMANCI_EQUIVALENCE_SCALE=full` for the wide local run). ### Changed
- Engine completion trie is now a compact flat-array trie (`engine/src/trie.rs`): one label, first-child index, child count and terminal index per node (16 bytes) instead of a hash table per node, word text stored once, children in code point order; tries are built explicitly after loading. Observable behaviour is unchanged and proven: a reference copy of the previous implementation is compared on every prefix of every word (`trie::equivalence_tests`, including the built packs), 756 golden CLI queries on the reviewed and experimental-full packs are byte-identical, and the 357-case `evaluate-packs` reports are byte-identical. Experimental-full engine heap 58.0 MB → 22.4 MB (trie 39 MB → 3 MB, RSS after load 77 MB → 46 MB), reviewed 2.84 MB → 1.45 MB; see `docs/memory-attribution.md`. `MemoryAttribution` reports `trie_node_arrays` and `trie_words` in place of `trie_child_tables` and `trie_word_copies`; the trie is built and enumerated with explicit stacks, so load and query depth never depend on the call stack (a valid pack may contain a word of tens of thousands of characters).
